# Community & Collaboration Guidelines

Welcome to the Snap-it community. Whether you are building custom filters, testing privacy invariants, or fixing bugs, we want this project to be a welcoming and productive space.

## Communication Channels

- **GitHub Discussions:** [Open a Discussion](https://github.com/MRINALPRAKASHFSD/snap-it/discussions) for feature ideas, architectural questions, and showing off camera filter combinations.
- **GitHub Issues:** [Browse Issues](https://github.com/MRINALPRAKASHFSD/snap-it/issues) to find bug reports, planned features, and good first issues.
- **Direct Maintainer Contact:** Reach out directly via `mrinalprakash9123@gmail.com`.

## How to Get Involved

1. **Test on Different Devices:** We need community testing across various hardware configurations—especially front/rear camera switching on diverse Android and iOS handsets.
2. **Submit Filter Presets:** Have an idea for a photographic look? Check out `src/lib/camera/filters.ts` and submit a PR with your CSS filter configuration.
3. **Audit Privacy Rules:** Help us verify that our zero-location invariant and storage signing rules remain unbreakable.
4. **Improve Documentation:** Found a typo or an unclear step in `DEVELOPMENT.md`? Documentation PRs are always welcome.

## Feedback Etiquette

- Be constructive and specific when reviewing code or discussing proposed changes.
- Focus on technical rationale and user experience.
- Respect everyone's time by providing reproduction steps for issues and context for feature requests.
- Adhere to the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) in all community interactions.
